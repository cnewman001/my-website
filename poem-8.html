Them

By Vala Newman

When I look at you I can see everything Our future our life the woman I want to be I can see me being a better person because of you Because I love you And your love in me is the greatest thing that has happened to me When I close my eyes I got to sleep smiling because you’re the last thing I see And when I wake up you’re the first thing I see You’re kisses feel like love when you press your lips on mine And your touch feels like heaven on my skin I never knew what an angel looked like till I look in your eyes
