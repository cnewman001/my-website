Fate, First Kisses & Name Stealing: Why This Time Next Year Captured My Heart

By Vala Newman | Category: Cinema & Movie Reviews

Quick Summary

Based on the novel by Sophie Cousens, This Time Next Year follows Minnie Cooper (Sophie Cookson) and Quinn Hamilton (Lucien Laviscount), who were born in the exact same London hospital on New Year's Day 1990—just one minute apart. Quinn's mother took the name, the media spotlight, and the cash prize meant for Minnie, sparking a lifelong belief that Minnie was cursed with bad luck. Decades later, a series of New Year's Eve encounters forces them together as they navigate failing businesses, complex family dynamics, and a love story that was written in the stars.

I just finished watching This Time Next Year on Prime, and it was such a fun watch! The cute name stealing rivalry paired with real emotional depth made this romantic comedy so charming.

The Stolen Name & Maternal Healing

The setup is hilarious, but what really pulled me in was how the story handled the dynamic between the mothers. Minnie grew up believing her birthday was jinxed because Quinn's mom, Tara, claimed the name Quinn and the cash prize at the hospital, leaving Minnie's mom, Connie, bitter over the loss. But seeing how much Tara struggled with her mental health softened Connie completely. Instead of holding onto a decades long grudge, the two mothers formed a genuine connection, softening towards each other and essentially stepping up to be the maternal support both of their children needed.

Overcoming the Jinx & Growth

Minnie's journey from believing the universe was out to get her to taking charge of her life was so satisfying. Watching Quinn drop his guarded exterior, help deliver pies for her business, and support her dreams showed how much care they built for each other. Their late night chats, heath swims, and honest conversations gave their bond a real foundation.

Great Build Up, But A Little Too Brief at the End

My main critique with the film is that they spent so much time on the build up, misunderstandings, and emotional obstacles that the actual romantic payout felt a bit rushed. After all that build up, we only got a short window to actually see them together as a couple. The lemming o'clock first kiss reveal was pure romance perfection, but I definitely wanted more time enjoying their actual romance after waiting the whole movie for it!

Final Verdict

With great chemistry between Sophie Cookson and Lucien Laviscount, charming London energy, and a deeply emotional payoff, This Time Next Year is a great rom com, even if it leaves you wishing the couple had a little more screen time together at the finish line.

Rating: 4/5 Stars
