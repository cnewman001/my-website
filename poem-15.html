To my father

By Vala Newman

This is for my dad thank you for being my first I love yous not many get to have that experience. Thank you for the many dates and nail shop appointments that over the year I forgot I deserve. Thank you for the many many after school activities from ballet to karate to acting to just being a kid. Thank you for the childhood you were able to give me even if it was taken away by outside forces.
Thank you for being my best friend my dad my diary my therapist and even the person that calls me out on the bs lput myself through. Thank you for loving me even when I don't necessarily love myself. lust thank you for being there thank you for the future I get to have thank you for the aisle you'll walk me down and the grandkids you'll get to meet. Thank you for not chasing off my future husband even if it means one day you'll have to let me grow up. Thank you for letting me grown up.
