l am just a girl

By Vala Newman

I am just a girl
I am just a girl| enjoy walks with music, I see the world in many colors
I am just a girl I read books that transport my into many worlds
In some l'm a lawyer in others I'm just a girl that hopes
I am just a girl| look for the best in everyone even when I'm surrounded by bad
I am just a girl I'm learning and growing from my mistakes
I am just a woman for l've blossomed into a new being
I am just a woman i have hair that flows, my beauty stops time and captivates the room
I am just a woman I still look for the best in everyone when I'm surrounded by the bad
I am just a woman I'm building a future that my kids can be proud of
I am just a woman
