The Voicemail

By Vala Newman

Hey I know we haven’t talked in a long time. I know it’s my fault I pushed you away. But I wanted to tell you that I miss you. I miss us. I miss what we had what we used to be. And I don’t know why things happened the way they did but they happened and I can’t take it back. I don’t know if we can fix this I don’t know if we’d be the same or if our friendship would go back to how it was. But I’m willing to try if you are. Because if you love something or someone you fight for them. So here I am leaving this voicemail fighting for us. After that fight we had I didn’t think you’d talk to me again. But the phone didn’t go straight to voicemail so here’s hope. That’s all I wanted to say if you get this message call me I miss you. Bye
