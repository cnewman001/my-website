Three Loves

By Vala Newman

There is a belief that we only fall in love with three people in our lives. Each love just as painful as the last. And each one for a very specific purpose. Your first love is the fairytale love. Happy. Hardest to let go. New. Exciting. Your second love is the learning love. Healing. Mature. Your third and final love is your lasting love. Strong. Connected. Takes you by surprise. Everlasting. Everyone wants happiness without any pain but, you can’t have a rainbow without any rain.
