A maybe

By Vala Newman

Sometimes I think I’m a definitely
Then others I feel like a maybe
Most of the time I am a what if
What if I was quieter
What if I was more confident
What if my anxiety didn’t keep my thoughts from flooding
I’d rather be a maybe than a what if
Because maybe I’d have a chance
Maybe I’d be happier
Maybe just maybe I’d be the one
It would be great to be a definitely but for now I’d settle for a maybe
