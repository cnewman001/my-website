      <h1 class="poem-title">To my best friend</h1>

      <p class="poem-author">By Vala Newman</p>


      <div class="poem-body">

        <p>

          You are a bright light stronger than most lights<br>

          And when people don’t know how to deal with something they try to dim that light<br>

          But you are so unique and different your light shines even more when people try to bring you down<br>

          You are a breath of fresh air while I never fit with anyone<br>

          We fit together perfectly you are half of my heart<br>

          If soulmates were platonic you’d be mine<br>

          I love you so much sometimes your pain hurts me more than it hurts you<br>

          Because I can’t understand why a beautiful soul as yours has so much pain<br>

          To my best friend you’re more than enough you are the world

        </p>
