To my best friend

By Vala Newman

You are a bright light stronger than most lights
And when people don’t know how to deal with something they try to dim that light
But you are so unique and different your light shines even more when people try to bring you down
You are a breath of fresh air while I never fit with anyone
We fit together perfectly you are half of my heart
If soulmates were platonic you’d be mine
I love you so much sometimes your pain hurts me more than it hurts you
Because I can’t understand why a beautiful soul as yours has so much pain
To my best friend you’re more than enough you are the world
