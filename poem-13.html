It was a day like no other

By Vala Newman

I woke up got dressed went about my day
But then I came in the house from the run to the store
They were all looking at each other who’s gonna break the news
My sister, my protector said your auntie isn’t here
I laughed thinking it was some stupid joke they believed was funny
No one laughed soon my laughs turned to tears as I fell on the bed broken once again
Another mother lost my aunt the lady I call when I need advice or to vent
The lady i introduce to all my relationships before anyone else
Wow how it hurt to know that every birthday I won’t get my calls where you sing loud at the top of your lungs
Today is my birthday I’m 23 we talked about my German chocolate cake you swore I would love
I tried so hard not too but as always auntie Trina was right it’s my favorite
Today is my birthday I’m 23 I thought you would be here with me
Where I can come to your house and say happy anniversary because you got married on my birthday
And you said it was your birthday present to me at the time I didn’t understand i was 21
But now I do it’s your present to me to live the fullest
Love the fullest have a day where it’s about me but it’s about me because you helped me get here
Anything I wanna achieve you helped me see I can
Today I am 23 years old and I am happy I am me I am kind I am a child of god
Today I am 23 and I miss you all the time
But today I am also 23 and I am full for life I get high on adventure
I get a rush in knowing I can make you happy
Auntie today it’s my birthday but this poem is my anniversary gift to you
Hey auntie it’s your niece Rawlene I’m 23 today and I miss you
