      <h1 class="poem-title">It was a day like no other</h1>

      <p class="poem-author">By Vala Newman</p>


      <div class="poem-body">

        <p>

          I woke up got dressed went about my day<br>

          But then I came in the house from the run to the store<br>

          They were all looking at each other who’s gonna break the news<br>

          My sister, my protector said your auntie isn’t here<br>

          I laughed thinking it was some stupid joke they believed was funny<br>

          No one laughed soon my laughs turned to tears as I fell on the bed broken once again<br>

          Another mother lost my aunt the lady I call when I need advice or to vent<br>

          The lady i introduce to all my relationships before anyone else<br>

          Wow how it hurt to know that every birthday I won’t get my calls where you sing loud at the top of your lungs<br>

          Today is my birthday I’m 23 we talked about my German chocolate cake you swore I would love<br>

          I tried so hard not too but as always auntie Trina was right it’s my favorite<br>

          Today is my birthday I’m 23 I thought you would be here with me<br>

          Where I can come to your house and say happy anniversary because you got married on my birthday<br>

          And you said it was your birthday present to me at the time I didn’t understand i was 21<br>

          But now I do it’s your present to me to live the fullest<br>

          Love the fullest have a day where it’s about me but it’s about me because you helped me get here<br>

          Anything I wanna achieve you helped me see I can<br>

          Today I am 23 years old and I am happy I am me I am kind I am a child of god<br>

          Today I am 23 and I miss you all the time<br>

          But today I am also 23 and I am full for life I get high on adventure<br>

          I get a rush in knowing I can make you happy<br>

          Auntie today it’s my birthday but this poem is my anniversary gift to you<br>

          Hey auntie it’s your niece Rawlene I’m 23 today and I miss you

        </p>
