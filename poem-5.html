Unknown

By Vala Newman

One day you will know my name You’ll know who I am where I come from My past my hurts You’ll know who I was and who I became My lover my family You’ll know my kids and their kids But for now I am unknown Just a girl who writes and listens to music Someone who likes to escape the world I am your friend or a stranger to you You’ll know who I am in the future But for now I am an unknown
