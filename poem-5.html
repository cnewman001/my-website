      <h1 class="poem-title">Unknown</h1>

      <p class="poem-author">By Vala Newman</p>


      <div class="poem-body">One day you will know my name

You’ll know who I am where I come from

My past my hurts

You’ll know who I was and who I became

My lover my family

You’ll know my kids and their kids

But for now I am unknown

Just a girl who writes and listens to music

Someone who likes to escape the world

I am your friend or a stranger to you

You’ll know who I am in the future 

But for now I am an unknown</div>
