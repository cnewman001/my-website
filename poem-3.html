Unexpressed feelings

By Vala Newman

Words have always been hard for me to use in the right way. I can’t tell you how I feel but I can write it. I feel good when I talk to you. I could have an attitude and the smallest text makes me smile instantly. You asked me how I feel this is it. I like when you do that small smile cause you see your dimples. I love hugging you cause you’re taller than me and you hold on for a moment. I like that I feel like I can talk to you about anything like my past or stuff that happens in that moment. I like you I just don’t know how to say it.
