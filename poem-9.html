Depression the silent film

By Vala Newman

To me depression is like a movie I didn’t wanna be casted in
Every where you turn it’s a different screen
Like the one where you sleep all day because the thought of waking up is too tiring
Or the one where you’re being called lazy because you are sad
Or the one where keeping all your feelings inside is better than letting anyone know you’d rather die than live because it’s easier
When did life get categorized as it should be easy
As a kid you can’t wait to grow up because everyone around you treats you like a burden instead of nurturing you
So you spend your days overwhelmed by the small things since no one took their time to teach it to you.
See in my household depression doesn’t exist if you’re sad so be productive you’ll be alright
If you wanna die you woke up this morning not many people did so why would you even say that
Not even asking why do you feel that way but immediately dismissing it by making you feel bad for even thinking your life is insignificant enough to have that thought
They give you things like suicide hotline numbers but uu call it and immediately gets put on hold like you aren’t thinking of taking your life in that moment
See they care about you until they have to deal with your burdens then you’re seen as crazy or too much
