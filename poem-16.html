I don't think l'm ready to love someone again.

By Vala Newman

I don't think l'm ready to love someone again. That being said we can still Face Time every night and go out on little dates as long as you promise you won't fall for me. Because I promise you wouldn't be able to handle it. I have a hard time explaining myself because I do don't fully understand my emotions. You can fall in love at your own risk but I'm telling you it's not advisable because I don't want to love again and I'll inevitably break your heart like the ones in the past have broken mines because now I'm not in the space to give you want you want. And unlike my ex| don't have the heart to string you along and act like I'm in love knowing at any moment I could ghost you. I don't think I'm ready to love someone again it's too hard when you're the only one falling and I can't fall first again but i also can't fall too late.
