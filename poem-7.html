Handle with Care

By Vala Newman

It wasn’t in my intention to like you It wasn’t on my list to get involved with anyone It wasn’t on the list of things I had planned for this summer But it happened I met you I liked you I got you Now I don’t wanna let go of you But if for any reason you feel like you wanna leave Tell me tell me how you feel and we can go our separate ways Don’t hurt me I’ve had enough pain to last a life time
